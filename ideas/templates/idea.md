## Seed

The original thought, exactly as it was captured. Do not edit it later. Later rounds show how the thinking moved away from it.

## Round 1 — YYYY-MM-DD

**My understanding** *(drafted, not yet confirmed)*: The idea in the owner's terms: what problem it addresses, for whom, and why it seems worth doing. Remove the drafted marker once the owner confirms or corrects it.

**Feedback:**

- **Restated:** the idea in one or two sentences, as the reviewer understood it.
- **Weakest assumption:** the belief that, if wrong, sinks the idea.
- **Questions:** one or two that move the thinking forward.
- **Prior art:** what already exists, and how this idea differs, or "none found".

**Recommendation:** Experiment | Another round | Park | Abandon. One line on why.

If the recommendation is Experiment:

- **Hypothesis:** what the experiment would show.
- **Smallest build:** the least code that tests it, in `experiments/<name>/`.
- **Time box:** how long before stopping to judge it.
- **Signal:** what result proves it, and what result kills it.

**What changed:** what the owner now thinks differently after this round. Leave it empty until the next conversation.

## Experiment

Filled in only when status is `Experimenting` or later. Link to `experiments/<name>/`, then record the result against the signal above.

## Verdict

Filled in when the idea is parked, abandoned, building, or shipped. Say why, and for `Parked`, what would bring the idea back.
