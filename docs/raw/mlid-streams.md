# My Life In Digital Stream

## Background

When using any of the AI chat apps such as Claude and ChatGPT I really enjoy using the short form. Like you think of something and you type it and the AI model responds. Being able to articulate ideas over a period of time with short ideas is useful. At times I simply do not have the mental energy to write a complex post or document. Ideas can shape over time or acts as input to other ideas. Or simply exist. 

Ideas or quick fire notes should not require too much ceremony to setup. It speaks to the way I have been using ChatGPT. Many thoughts are one-liners that I can choose to expand on. And it comes from always enjoying general knowledge. Not every one-liner has to explode into some magnificent app idea - sometimes I really want to know what the size difference between a Siberian and Bengal Tiger is. 

What if I can create a local tool or utility to facilitate the short-form I like so much? The workflow could be:

- capture idea or note
- use an LLM wiki skill to index the generated content.
  - indexing can use a sqlite db
  - also maintain an index and log 
- one of the key features is to categorize content using an AI integration. I do not want to have to organize my notes manually. so when I capture another note or idea the LLM can detect patterns and organize it for me. 
- it could also provide feedback on my notes

A crucial part is that it somehow feeds into the content creation and publishing. Ideally I will need to go through some process to define the workflows. But I do not want to have to define schemas and templates for every "content type".

The end goal is to have some sort of tool that lets me capture ideas with the integration of a harness such as Codex. I want the ability to use the harness as a publication and content authoring assistant. 

But what does it showcase for My Life In Digital?

One of the key intentions with My Life In Digital is showcasing experiments. Thoughts, ideas that lead to experiments. So do I gear the content creation toward the experimentation? I have also realized that the My Life In Digital landing page is more like a catalogue. 

If I think about it I have been experimenting with story creation using "Story crafter". Golden Valley is into season 9 already. And my feeling with the story crafter experimentation is that I could potentially extract the central building blocks for developing stories. World bibles, character progression etc. 

I have experimented with content creation using Markdown. Although this is not really an area of experimentation as its a content repository. 

I also have the My Life In Print rework in the pipeline.
