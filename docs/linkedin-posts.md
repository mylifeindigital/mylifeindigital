## WEEK 1 – Set the tone (no announcements)

**TUE:**
**Topic:** *“I stopped optimizing for outcomes. I optimize for showing up.”*

* Talk about consistency over ambition
* Mention MyLifeInDigital casually (no pitch)

**THU:**
**Topic:** *What I’m building right now (and why it’s intentionally small)*

I’m curious by nature — I like to try things.
It applies to many areas of my life, from making food to writing code.

The only real way to know if something works is to try it.
I’m learning more by poking at small things than by finishing big ones.
Progress beats perfection, and looking back at how far you’ve come is surprisingly motivating.

I spent a short session today digging into how Markdown parsing actually works in Node.js.

Not building anything impressive.
Just stepping away from “parse Markdown → get HTML” and looking at the tokens underneath.

I’m also exploring better ways to handle different types of posts in a Hono app — where different content types can be rendered to create different experiences.

The interesting part wasn’t the tooling.
It was realizing how easy it is to learn how something works — and how much harder it is to be clear on why you’re learning it.

I’ve noticed that understanding usually comes from cutting things down to their simplest form.
Small scripts help here. Start with a console.log, then intentionally add complexity.

Today felt slow. A bit fuzzy.
But it clarified the direction.

Progress sometimes looks like confusion followed by showing up again.
