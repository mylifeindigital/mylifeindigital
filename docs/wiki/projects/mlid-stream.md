# MLID Stream

An exploratory local tool for capturing short thoughts with little ceremony, then using AI to organize them and support content authoring. A note can remain a one-liner or general-knowledge question; it need not become a post or application idea.

## Intended Workflow

- Capture an idea or note without requiring a schema or template for every content type.
- Use an LLM wiki skill to index the captured material and maintain an index and log. SQLite is a possible indexing mechanism, not a selected storage architecture.
- Let AI detect patterns and categorize notes instead of requiring manual organization. Feedback on notes is another possible capability.
- Connect capture to content creation and publishing through workflows still to be defined, with a harness such as Codex acting as an authoring and publication assistant.

## Relationship to My Life In Digital

The source asks whether the site should foreground experiments emerging from thoughts and ideas; it describes the landing page as feeling more like a catalogue. This is a direction to explore, not an approved site redesign.

Story Crafter is an example of experimentation: the note reports Golden Valley reaching season 9 and suggests extracting reusable story-building elements such as world bibles and character progression. It also mentions a My Life In Print rework without defining its scope.

## Open Boundaries

- How does free-form capture connect to the existing template-driven [Content Operations App](./content-operations-app.md)? The source's preference against per-type schemas and templates is in tension with that workflow; neither direction supersedes the other here.
- Should experiments guide how captured notes become public content, and what should remain private or unexpanded?
- What would a local shell app look like? [Bun Shell](../concepts/bun-shell.md) is a candidate reference, not a runtime decision or a change to the repository's npm workflow.
- What role would SQLite play alongside the index and log, and where would canonical notes live?
